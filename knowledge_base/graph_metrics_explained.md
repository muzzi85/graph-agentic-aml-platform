# Graph Metrics Explained

Degree Centrality:

High degree centrality indicates
large transaction connectivity.

This may indicate:
- money mule behavior
- transaction aggregation
- suspicious hubs

Betweenness Centrality:

High betweenness centrality indicates
an account acts as an intermediary
between many transaction paths.

This may indicate:
- layering activity
- laundering routing
- intermediary laundering behavior

PageRank:

High PageRank indicates
important graph influence.

Clustering Coefficient:

High clustering may indicate
coordinated suspicious groups.
