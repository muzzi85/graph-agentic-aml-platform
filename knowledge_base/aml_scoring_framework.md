# AML Scoring Framework

AML scores are normalized between 0 and 1.

Interpretation:

- 0.0 to 0.3:
  Low AML risk

- 0.3 to 0.7:
  Medium AML risk

- Above 0.7:
  High AML risk

High AML scores indicate:

- suspicious transaction behavior
- abnormal graph connectivity
- elevated suspicious neighbor exposure
- possible laundering topology

Accounts above 0.7 require:
- AML escalation
- compliance review
- enhanced investigation
